# Inventory and Reorder Guidelines

## Reorder Point
A product is flagged for reorder when its on-hand quantity at a store is at or below its reorder point. The weekly replenishment order is submitted every Monday by the inventory lead.

## Cycle Counts
High-value items, defined as a unit cost of 100 dollars or more, are cycle counted every week. All other items are cycle counted once per quarter.

## Shrink Adjustments
Inventory adjustments for shrink, damage, or theft above 200 dollars in total value require approval from the district manager. Adjustments at or below 200 dollars can be approved by the store manager.
