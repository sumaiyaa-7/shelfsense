# Cash Handling Standard Operating Procedure

## Opening and Closing Counts
Every cash drawer is counted at the start and end of each shift by the assigned cashier, and the counts are recorded in the drawer log. Each drawer starts with a float of 150 dollars.

## Variances
A drawer variance of up to 5 dollars over or short is within tolerance and is noted in the drawer log. A variance greater than 5 dollars requires a manager sign-off and an incident report filed within 24 hours. Three out-of-tolerance variances in 30 days triggers a coaching conversation with the store manager.

## Counterfeit Checks
All bills of 50 dollars or more must be checked with a counterfeit detection pen. Suspected counterfeit bills are not returned to the customer and are reported to the manager on duty.

## Safe Drops
Cash above 300 dollars in a drawer is moved to the safe through a safe drop. Safe counts always require two people, a manager and a second associate.
