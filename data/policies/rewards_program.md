# Rewards Program

## Earning Rewards
Rewards members earn 5 percent back in rewards on most purchases and 10 percent back on ink and toner. Rewards are issued monthly on the 15th and appear in the member's account and app.

## Expiration
Issued rewards expire 90 days after they are issued. Expired rewards cannot be reinstated by store associates.

## Ink Recycling
Members earn 2 dollars in rewards for each recycled ink or toner cartridge, up to 10 cartridges per month. To earn recycling rewards, the member must have spent at least 10 dollars on ink or toner in the previous 180 days.

## Signing Up
Membership is free. An associate can enroll a customer at checkout using only a phone number and email address.
