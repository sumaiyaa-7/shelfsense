# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard Returns
Most items can be returned within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt for a full refund to the original form of payment. Rewards members can look up receipts by phone number, so a missing paper receipt is not a problem for members.

## Electronics and Technology
Opened laptops, tablets, monitors, and printers must be returned within 14 days of purchase. Opened printers and 3D printers carry a 15 percent restocking fee unless the item is defective. Unopened technology follows the standard 30 day window.

## Returns Without a Receipt
Returns without a receipt are refunded as store credit at the lowest selling price in the last 90 days. A valid government photo ID is required. A customer may make at most 3 no-receipt returns in any 12 month period.

## Final Sale Items
Gift cards, items marked clearance final sale, opened software, and personalized print orders cannot be returned. Defective final sale items may be exchanged for the same item.
