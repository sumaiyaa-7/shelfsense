# Online Order Pickup and Substitution Policy

## Buy Online Pick Up In Store
Pickup orders are held for 5 days after the customer is notified that the order is ready. After 5 days, uncollected orders are cancelled, items are returned to stock, and the customer is refunded automatically.

## Substitutions
When an ordered item is out of stock, the digital shopper may offer a substitute of the same type at equal or greater value, charged at the original item's price. The customer must approve the substitution in the app within 2 hours. If the customer does not respond within 2 hours, the item is removed from the order and refunded.

## Partial Fulfillment
If some items cannot be filled or substituted, the order is partially fulfilled and the customer receives an email listing the missing items and the refund amount.
