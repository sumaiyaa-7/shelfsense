# Price Match Guarantee

## Eligibility
We match the current price of an identical item sold by a qualifying competitor, either in store or on the competitor's own website. The item must be the same brand, model number, color, and size, and it must be in stock at the competitor.

## Exclusions
Price matching does not apply to third-party marketplace sellers, clearance or open-box items, bundle offers, membership-only prices, or limited-time doorbuster deals. Price matching is limited to 5 units per item per customer per day.

## Price Adjustments After Purchase
If our own price drops within 14 days of purchase, a customer can request a one-time price adjustment for the difference. The original receipt or a rewards account lookup is required for a price adjustment.
